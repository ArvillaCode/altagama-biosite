## Escena inferior

`footer-objects-clean.webp`: edición y composición mediante la herramienta integrada ImageGen, a partir de las imágenes existentes de gorra, gafas y roca. Fondo transparente. No contiene retratos incrustados.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: compositing / precise-object-edit. Create one clean photorealistic footer asset for the existing ALTAGAMA streetwear microsite. The three references are edit targets and object references: black BALENCIAGA baseball cap, black Ray-Ban sunglasses, dark stone engraved ALTA GAMA STORE. Retain their black fabrics, metallic gold accents and warm gold studio lighting. Compose a horizontal wide arrangement: cap on left, sunglasses in lower foreground left/center, rough engraved stone on right. Complete the missing object edges cleanly. Engraving should read ALTA GAMA STORE. Cap embroidery BALENCIAGA; tiny Ray-Ban signature on sunglasses. Absolutely REMOVE ALL PEOPLE, faces, circular portrait thumbnails, review cards, text snippets, phone UI and backgrounds from the supplied cropped references. Output only these three objects on genuine transparent alpha background, soft realistic shadows directly under objects. Wide horizontal 3:1 composition suited to a website footer, all objects fully visible, tasteful luxury urban product photography. Do not include any person or floating head anywhere.
